"Dendroscopy" is the study of the tree structure of BPS bound states. For the
local Calabi–Yau $\mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ we tracked the full BPS
spectrum across three regimes — the orbifold point, the large-volume limit, and
the $\Pi$-stability slice between them — using **scattering diagrams** whose rays
encode stable charges.

Much of the work was making the picture consistent: enforcing ray-intersection
constraints, and extending the $\Gamma_0(4)$ monodromy by a $\mathbb{Z}_4$
symmetry so the diagram closes up correctly. I wrote the Mathematica tooling that
generates the figures and tables and checks these constraints, which turned a
delicate hand computation into something reproducible.
