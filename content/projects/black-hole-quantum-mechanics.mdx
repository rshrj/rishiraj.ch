Denef's $n$-centre $\mathcal{N}=4$ supersymmetric quantum mechanics models a
collection of BPS black holes bound together on the Coulomb branch. Its refined
Witten index counts the bound states — but the naive count is _not_ modular,
which is a problem if you want it to match a black-hole partition function.

Using **localization**, we reduced the index to a Coulomb-branch integral and
evaluated it explicitly. The subtle part is the completion: the holomorphic
answer must be corrected by a non-holomorphic piece, built from **generalized
error functions**, to restore modularity. We checked the construction up to four
centres. It is a clean example of a pattern I keep meeting — a count that only
makes sense once you add the term that makes it transform correctly.
